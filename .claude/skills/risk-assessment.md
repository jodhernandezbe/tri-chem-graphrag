# Risk Assessment Skill

## Chemical Risk Assessment Framework
- **Hazard Identification**: Chemical toxicity and health effects
- **Exposure Assessment**: Release quantities, pathways, populations
- **Dose-Response Assessment**: Toxicity thresholds and safety factors
- **Risk Characterization**: Integration of hazard and exposure data

## Source-to-Receptor Analysis
- Track chemical releases from TRI facilities to environmental media
- Model transport and fate in air, water, soil
- Identify exposure pathways (inhalation, ingestion, dermal)
- Characterize receptor populations and vulnerabilities

## TRI Data Integration
- Link release quantities to exposure estimates
- Analyze temporal trends in chemical releases
- Compare across industrial sectors and geographic regions
- Identify high-priority chemicals and facilities

## Risk Metrics
- Calculate cancer risk estimates
- Assess non-cancer health hazards
- Environmental quotients for ecological risk
- Cumulative risk from multiple chemicals

## Uncertainty Analysis
- Quantify uncertainty in exposure estimates
- Sensitivity analysis for key parameters
- Monte Carlo simulation for probabilistic risk
- Quality assurance for risk calculations

## Source Reduction Analysis
- **TRI Form 2a Data**: Track pollution prevention activities and effectiveness
- **P2 Strategies**: Analyze process modifications, input substitution, recycling
- **Quantitative Assessment**: Measure annual reduction amounts and trends
- **Industry Benchmarking**: Compare source reduction across sectors and facilities
- **Cost-Benefit Analysis**: Evaluate economic efficiency of reduction strategies

## Alternatives Assessment
- Identify safer chemical substitutes via structural similarity
- Evaluate relative risk trade-offs between chemicals
- Support pollution prevention and green chemistry decisions
- Assess feasibility of chemical substitution in industrial processes
- Link source reduction data to exposure and risk reductions
