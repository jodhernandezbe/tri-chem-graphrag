# Environmental Chemistry Skill

## Chemical Fate and Transport
- Partition coefficients (Kow, Koc) for environmental distribution
- Bioaccumulation potential (BCF, BAF) in food chains
- Persistence assessment (biodegradation, photolysis, hydrolysis)
- Long-range transport potential for global contaminants

## Environmental Media
- **Air**: Vapor pressure, Henry's law constant, atmospheric reactions
- **Water**: Solubility, pH effects, aquatic chemistry
- **Soil**: Sorption, leaching potential, soil chemistry
- **Biota**: Bioconcentration, biomagnification, metabolism

## Chemical Properties for Risk Assessment
- Physical-chemical properties from SMILES structures
- QSAR models for missing environmental data
- Structure-activity relationships for toxicity
- Environmental classification schemes

## Pollution Sources and Pathways
- Industrial point sources (TRI facilities)
- Non-point source contributions
- Atmospheric deposition patterns
- Groundwater and surface water contamination

## Transformation Products
- Metabolite identification and tracking
- Environmental degradation pathways
- Formation of more/less toxic products
- Analytical method requirements

## Green Chemistry Integration
- Benign by design principles
- Safer chemical alternatives identification
- Life cycle assessment integration
- Pollution prevention strategies
